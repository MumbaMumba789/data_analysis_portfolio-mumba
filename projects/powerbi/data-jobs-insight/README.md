# Data Jobs Insight — Power BI Dashboard

**What this is:** A multi-page Power BI dashboard analyzing data
analytics job postings — pay, platforms, companies, and in-demand
skills — with a drill-through page for deeper skill-level detail.

---

## Overview page — Data Jobs Insight

Key numbers across the dataset: **$50.3** hourly average salary,
**9,088** total companies, **$127.6K** yearly average salary, **167K**
total job postings, and **221** total skills tracked.

- **Highest paying companies** — Capital One and Hanmham top the list,
  both above $145K average yearly salary, well ahead of the rest of
  the field.
- **Job average salary by title** — Senior Data Scientist and Senior
  Data Engineer roles lead at $152K and $148K, with the average
  across all titles landing at $127.55K.
- **Platform job postings** — LinkedIn dominates sourcing volume at
  32.2K postings, more than the next several platforms combined.
- **Health insurance vs. pay** — Roles offering health insurance pay
  noticeably more on average ($135K) than those without ($122K).

## Drill-through page — Skills Drill Through

- **Most demanded skills** — SQL and Python are far ahead of every
  other skill in job posting volume.
- **Does a degree pay more?** — Compares salary by skill split between
  postings that require a degree and those that don't.
- **Job openings vs. hourly salary** — Scatter view positioning each
  skill by demand (openings) against pay, highlighting where
  high-demand and high-pay skills overlap (e.g. SQL, Python) versus
  skills that are well-paid but lower-volume.
- **Full skills table** — Job postings, median hourly rate, and yearly
  average salary for every skill tracked, sortable.
