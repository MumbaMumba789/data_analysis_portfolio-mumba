# Sales Visualisation Dashboard

**What this is:** A Power BI dashboard tracking order shipping
performance and revenue trends, with a year filter (2017–2020) and a
breakdown of revenue by order-size ("Cookie Range") bucket.

---

## Dashboard

- **KPI cards** — Average Revenue ($2.5K, above target), Orders
  Shipped (788), Total Orders (788), Open Orders (0, 0% open rate),
  and Average Shipping Time (4 days).
- **Average cost and profit by month** — Side-by-side comparison
  across all 12 months, with September as the highest month for both.
- **Average revenue by month** — Trend line showing revenue easing
  from a peak of $2.7K down to $2.3K over the year, with a tooltip
  detail view (e.g. September: $2,678.60 average revenue).
- **Average revenue by Cookie Range** — Orders bucketed by size
  (0–200, 200–400, 400–600, 600–800, 800+), showing revenue increases
  sharply with order size — the 800+ bucket drives roughly 4x the
  revenue of the 200–400 bucket.
