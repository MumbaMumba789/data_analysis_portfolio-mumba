# Coffee Sales Performance Dashboard

**What this is:** A Power BI dashboard analyzing coffee shop sales
performance across revenue, order timing, product mix, and store
location, with a drill-through to per-store detail.

---

## Dashboard

- **KPI cards** — Total Revenue $699K, Profit $699K, Total Orders
  149K, Total Items Sold 214K, and Best Store: Hell's Kitchen.
- **Revenue trend by month** — Steady growth from $82K in January to
  $166K in June, with the steepest jump between February and April.
- **Total orders by hour** — Clear morning rush: orders peak at 10 AM
  (18,545) after ramping up from 6 AM, then decline through the
  afternoon and evening.
- **Total revenue by product category** — Coffee leads by a wide
  margin at $270K, followed by Tea ($196K) and Bakery ($82K); Branded,
  Loose Tea, Flavours, and Packaged Chocolate are the smallest
  categories.
- **Total orders by store location** — Hell's Kitchen and Astoria are
  roughly tied at 51K orders each, with Lower Manhattan close behind
  at 48K.
